Real Talk. Real Help.

Looking for a job in a new country is hard. You're applying to dozens of roles, hearing nothing back, and starting to lose hope.

That's exactly why NexHire Consultancy UK exists — to help you stop feeling stuck and start seeing tangible results in your career.

Who We Are?

At NexHire, we believe that behind every CV, every application, and every career goal is a real person with dreams, struggles, and hopes for a better future.

We know how difficult life can feel when you're doing everything you can, yet still feel stuck, confused, or left behind. The constant pressure to succeed, the fear of failure, the uncertainty about what comes next — it can become emotionally draining. Many people silently carry that weight every single day, and that's exactly why NexHire was created.

Not just to help people find opportunities, but to remind them that they still have potential, purpose, and a path forward.

Whether you're an international student trying to build a life away from home, balancing responsibilities and expectations, or a professional searching for clarity, growth, or a fresh beginning, we want you to know that your journey matters.

At NexHire, we are more than a career support platform. We are a team that genuinely cares about helping people rebuild confidence, discover direction, and take meaningful steps toward the future they deserve. Through honest guidance, practical support, and personal encouragement, we stand beside you at every stage of your journey.

Because sometimes, when someone finally believes in you and shows you the right path, everything begins to change.

Meet the People Behind NexHire

Sakshi Chauhan — Founder

Driven by a passion for helping people build successful careers, Sakshi founded NexHire with a vision to make career support more personal, supportive, and impactful. She believes that every individual deserves the right guidance, confidence, and opportunities to achieve their goals.

With hands-on experience in career mentoring, CV building, interview preparation, and candidate support, Sakshi has helped aspiring professionals take meaningful steps toward better opportunities and brighter futures. Her approach focuses not only on professional growth, but also on empowering people to believe in themselves throughout the journey.

At NexHire, she continues to create an environment where candidates feel valued, motivated, and fully supported at every stage of their career path.

“ When our candidates grow, succeed, and achieve their goals, we grow with them — and that's the energy that drives everything we do.”

PLACES OUR MEMBERS MAKE IT TO

Industries Our Members Come From

At NexHire Consultancy, we proudly support individuals from diverse academic and professional backgrounds. From technology and healthcare to finance, engineering, and hospitality, our members bring unique skills, ambitions, and experiences — and we help them transform those strengths into meaningful career opportunities across the UK.

Functional Management Industry

Our members step into dynamic roles across management, marketing, operations, and business coordination. From Project Managers and Marketing Executives to Operations Coordinators and Team Leads, they gain valuable industry exposure while developing leadership, communication, and strategic management skills in professional UK workplaces.

Healthcare & Laboratory Industry

We support aspiring healthcare and laboratory professionals in building impactful careers across hospitals, clinics, and diagnostic centers in the UK. Our members contribute to patient care, laboratory operations, clinical support, and public health services through both clinical and non-clinical roles.

Data & IT Industry

Our members build successful careers in fast-growing technology and analytics sectors. From Data Analysts and Software Developers to Business Analysts and Power BI Specialists, they help organizations drive innovation, improve decision-making, and leverage modern digital solutions across industries in the UK.

Finance Industry

NexHire members pursue rewarding opportunities in banking, accounting, investment, and financial operations. Through hands-on experience in financial reporting, analysis, bookkeeping, and client relationship management, they develop strong industry expertise within the UK finance sector.

Administrative & Support Industry

Our members contribute to the smooth functioning of businesses through roles in administration, HR, payroll, operations, and office coordination. They play a key role in supporting organizational efficiency, communication, and day-to-day business operations across multiple industries.

Hospitality & Customer Service Industry

From hotels and restaurants to corporate customer support environments, our members excel in customer-facing and operational roles. They gain valuable experience in guest relations, event coordination, customer support, and hospitality management while delivering exceptional service standards in the UK.

Cybersecurity Industry

Our members enter the growing field of cybersecurity through roles focused on protecting systems, networks, and sensitive data. From SOC Analysts to Security Coordinators and IT Security Trainees, they support organizations in maintaining digital safety and responding to evolving cyber threats.

Engineering & Technical Industry

NexHire supports engineering professionals across mechanical, civil, electrical, aerospace, automotive, and production sectors. Our members contribute to innovative projects involving design, testing, maintenance, manufacturing, and operational support across diverse engineering industries in the UK.

Supply Chain & Logistics Industry

Our members play a vital role in procurement, logistics, planning, inventory management, and distribution operations. Through careers in supply chain and logistics, they help organizations maintain efficient movement of goods, services, and resources across local and global networks.
